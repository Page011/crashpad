# Vera Crashpad privacy policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026
Applies to: Vera Crashpad (crashpad) for Windows, published by Page011
Contact: https://github.com/Page011/crashpad/issues

## Brief

Vera Crashpad is free and works entirely offline. It has no account system, no analytics, no
advertising and no server. It never sends your data anywhere, and the developer never receives it
and cannot see it.

## What it keeps on your PC

To do its job, the app saves these on your own PC and nowhere else:

- Clipboard history: text, images and file references you copy. Copies from password managers
  are skipped.
- Notes, as Markdown files in `Documents\crashpad`.
- Reminders, timers, the list of files parked on the Shelf (references, not copies) and your
  settings.
- Marked-up screenshots and zips, but only when you choose to save one.

It reads your Screenshots folder to show it, and reads system information (what's playing, CPU,
memory, battery, volume) to show on the Live tab. Text recognition (OCR) uses Windows' built-in
offline engine. None of this leaves your PC.

## What it does not do

- No network connections. Links in your notes open in your own browser when you click them.
- No telemetry, crash reporting, usage analytics or advertising identifiers.
- No sale or sharing of personal information. The developer never receives any.

The interface is drawn by Microsoft Edge WebView2, part of Windows, which is covered by
Microsoft's own privacy statement.

## Your control

Clear clipboard history, notes and reminders in the app at any time. Uninstalling removes the
app's data. Your notes in `Documents\crashpad` stay until you delete them.

Because the developer holds no personal information, there is nothing to request, correct or
erase under the GDPR, the CCPA, the Australian Privacy Act 1988 or any other law.

## Children

Vera Crashpad is a general productivity tool, not directed at children under 13. The developer
does not knowingly collect information from anyone, of any age.
